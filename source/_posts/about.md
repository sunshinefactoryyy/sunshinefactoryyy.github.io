I’m Wu Yuewei, a Final Year Information Security undergraduate at the National University of Singapore with a passion for understanding how systems work, uncovering their weaknesses, and making them more secure. My interests span vulnerability research, reverse engineering, and security operations, with a particular focus on IoT and firmware security.

Through roles at Bank of America, HTX, and Singtel, I’ve gained experience investigating security threats, researching hardware and firmware vulnerabilities, and managing enterprise security systems. My research has led to two published CVEs affecting TP-Link devices. I also actively compete in Capture the Flag competitions, with highlights including winning the DEFCON 33 Red Team Village CTF and earning a DEFCON Black Badge as part of NUS Greyhats with HTX.

Beyond technical work, I enjoy bringing people together to learn and explore cybersecurity. As Vice President of NUS Greyhats and the ISC2 Singapore Chapter Youth Wing, I help organise events, support fellow students, and mentor committee members. I’m driven by curiosity, practical problem-solving, and the opportunity to contribute to a stronger cybersecurity community.
